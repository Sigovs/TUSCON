About this car

In the owner’s words

Always Garaged
This car has been stored in a climate-controlled garage under a premium car cover, entirely shielded from weather, UV rays, and moisture.
Exterior
The factory two-tone Dark Purple Metallic and Arctic White paint shines like new. The original, bold Indy 500 door graphics and red accent pinstripes remain perfectly intact and crisp with no fading or cracking. There are a couple small flaws that I’ve taken close up pics of in the gallery.
Interior
The unique black leather sport seats with vibrant purple inserts are in pristine condition with virtually no bolster wear. The headrests feature the beautifully embroidered official Indy 500 logos.
Top
The original white vinyl soft top is in good shape, operates smoothly, and seals very well as an original should.
Engine
Numbers-matching 5.7L LT1 V8 producing 300 horsepower. It starts immediately, runs flawlessly, and sounds exactly as it did leaving the showroom floor.
Transmission
4-speed automatic transmission with the factory-ordered 3.07 performance rear axle ratio.
Wheels/Tires
Iconic 17-inch silver-painted 5-spoke aluminum wheels.
Options
Fully loaded with the mandatory Preferred Equipment Group, including electronic climate control, dual power seats, and the premium Delco/Bose stereo system.

Clean accident free Carfax, original owner’s manuals, selling Dealer plate, and original keys included.

New Michelin Pilot Sport tires were installed less than 30 miles ago in 6/ 2023,
