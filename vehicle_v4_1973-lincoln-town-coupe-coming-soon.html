About this car

In the owner’s words

1973 Lincoln Town coupe with only 25,047 miles since new. Optioned as a Premium town coupe and factory ordered in code 1c “Raven Black” and GA code Black Leather.

This car had an Show quality repaint in its original color of Raven Black with a very high level of detail. Door jambs are as nice as the exterior and the car was fully sanded and Buffed to a mirror finish. All door, hood, and trunk gaps are excellent. The undercarriage retains all of its original floors, rockers, and trunk and reflect the condition of a true low mile vehicle.

All exterior chrome and brightwork are in excellent condition. Windshield was replaced with a new one sourced from AGP, all other glass is original and in very good condition. The Vinyl top is original and in excellent condition minus one scratch on the Driver side C Pillar (pictured) The hide away headlight work great. All exterior lighting works.

The interior was completely redone in high grade Leather and appears like new. Some custom touches were added like the two tone with camel Brown and Black, hand embroidered Lincoln Logos on seats and door panels. Very Well done. Power seats work great, AC blows cold, heat works, wipers work, AM FM stereo works as well. Power door locks function as intended and it has a trunk release in the glove box. Power windows all work great. All of the weatherstripping and seals are in very good condition. Trunk has been completely refinished and comes with a set of floormats. It has a supoer cool cup holder in forward console, the doors flip open when you turn the knob forward, glove box door opens when you turn the other way. The dashboard has no crack and is in amazing condition. Door panels are beautiful.

Powered by a 460 V8 and C-6 automatic that at a minimum were removed from the car and resealed/ painted. Runs and drives amazing and the 460 just purrs. AC has been upgraded to R134a refrigerant. The under hood is fully detailed to show quality standards. The car is optioned with power steering and Power disc Brakes.

Rolls On fully polished Factory Lincoln 15” aluminum Rims and Michelin Radials. I have seen a few of these era Lincolns but NEVER one restored to this level. True collector grade quality and you will certainly not find one nicer at any price.
