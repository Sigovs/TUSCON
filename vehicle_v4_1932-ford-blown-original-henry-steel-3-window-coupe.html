About this car

In the owner’s words

1932 Ford 3 window coupe. Original Hotrod and real Henry Ford Steel body. We don’t have early history on the car but its been a Hot Rod for a very long time. The last owners had the car for over 25 years.

The last cosmetic work was performed in the 1970’s when it was painted Dark red Metallic with Gold velour interior. Very nice chop was performed sometime before that, its at least had 4” removed and looks killer with the angled A pillars. It has a smoothed cowl, roof insert, and frenched antenna. (the vent tray is still intact under cowl) All the garnish moulding are originals that were chopped to fit. Doors and decklid fit nice.

Chassis is a modified original with dropped front tube axle and adjustable 4 bar suspension. Rear is a GM 12 Bolt with transverse leaf spring and Ladder Bars. Engine was built about 15 yeas ago by previous owners who were avid drag racers. Built for street use, it runs amazing on pump gas, starts easy, and is super crisp when you hit the throttle…ts Rarin to go!! Built with an early 70’s 350, BDS 671 Blower, Pro Form carbs, electronic ignition, Edelbrock finned aluminum Carbs, and shorty headers with full stainless exhaust. Backed by a th350 automatic with shift kit. Disc brakes up front with drum in back.

Interior is a mix of the 70s velour interior with upgraded 1965 mustang buckets and black vinyl door panels. Sun gauges adorn the roadster dash with a SW Tach on the column drop. Windshield still flips out. Trunk is finished out from the 70’s in velour.

Lots of Patina all over, this baby has been around! Real deal Hot rod that is lots of fun to drive, turns heads wherever it goes. Don’t buy that plastic junk, own a real Hot Rod!
