About this bike

In the owner’s words

  • Kenny Eggers West Coast Championship winning motorcycle, frame no. 101
  • Motorcycle no. 1 of only 4 BSA factory 500cc rigid twin race bikes
  • This bike placed 5th in BSA’s 1st-5th sweep of the 1954 Daytona 200 race
  • Original lightweight duplex twin cylinder Dark Green racing frame with polychromatic Green cycle parts
  • Rare original 6-fin extra-thick flange competition barrel
  • Original BSA “DAY-T” Daytona 4-speed manual gearbox
  • Special construction race seat with under seat float chamber mounts
  • Smiths RC 83 tachometer
  • Special construction rigid oil tank with wing top cap
  • Special construction fuel tank with offset Monza cap
  • Special construction front mud guard mount
  • Special construction aluminum top triple tree
  • Original Dunlop flanged alloy rim spoked wheels
  • Twin right-hand swept header pipes with reverse cone megaphone mufflers
  • Kenny Eggers’ original racing number 59 on the front and side plates
  • Comprehensive restoration with attention to original factory specifications by multiple award winning restorer Thomas Weitacher
  • Includes original Cycle and American Motorcycle magazines featuring this bike
